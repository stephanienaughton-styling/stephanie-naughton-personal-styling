How to find shoulder width

Press your fingertip along the hard area on top of your shoulder and walk it outwards. The bone is firm and will not sink in. Once you reach a part that sinks in a little and feels softer, that is your point. Measure from this point to the same point on your other shoulder, in a very slightly curved line across the top of your shoulders.

Sizes by retailer

It's very helpful to know what size you wear in different retailers. For example, a size 12 in M&S is very different from a size 12 in Zara. Equally, if the size of your upper and lower body differs, please provide details (e.g. “I'm a 16 in tops from Zara but a 12 in bottoms”). Likewise with your shoe size: if you are in between sizes, please let me know.
